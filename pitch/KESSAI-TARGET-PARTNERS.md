# Kessai — Target Channel Partners

**Companies that would license Kessai (the commercial layer of AgentLevy) to add cryptographic-evidence auditability to their existing compliance / audit / escrow workflows.**

The pattern: each of these already has signed contracts with regulated buyers (banks, law firms, insurance, healthcare). Kessai becomes the cert-chain layer they upsell — they keep the customer relationship; we provide the verifiable-work substrate.

Ordered by likelihood of being an early licensee.

## Pitch language — using this list

If a judge / VC / partner asks **"who are your customers?"**:

> *"Compliance vendors who already sell to banks and law firms — Persona, Jumio, NetDocuments, iManage. Their customers are starting to demand cryptographic evidence rather than vendor-trusted logs. Kessai is the layer they license to add that without rebuilding their workflow. We're talking to the legal channel first because court-admissibility under FRE 902(13) is the most concrete near-term unlock."*

If they ask **"why not sell direct to banks?"**:

> *"Banks have 18-month sales cycles and procurement frameworks built around incumbent compliance vendors. Channel-licensing through those incumbents is faster: they get a defensive feature, we get distribution into their installed base. Direct-to-bank is the Phase 2 motion once we have proof points."*

## Why this list, structurally

Three filters drove the ordering:

1. **API-first** (faster integration, lower deal-sales-engineering friction)
2. **Already selling into compliance buyers** (no education needed — buyer already wants evidence layer)
3. **Modern enough to ship a new feature in months, not years** (skip the entrenched 1990s-architecture banking cores until Phase 2)

The Tier 1–2 list is roughly **20 logos** — that's a 12–18-month outbound plan with realistic conversion to 3–5 paying channel partners.
